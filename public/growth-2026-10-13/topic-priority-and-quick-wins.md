By Arhum Waheed. The 6 already-drafted guides (bingo, cold calling, spinner randomness, balanced teams, fair giveaway, Secret Santa) are excluded from this list.

## Research method
- Google autocomplete (US, en) pulled 2026-10-13. The raw strings are below.
- Live SERP checks for the top 2 topics. White Elephant: partycam.app, 101planners, joinue, mygiftcompass and whiteelephantrules.com, all small or niche sites with no big-media lock. Holiday raffle: simplyraffle, eventwrist blog, two r/office and r/GiftIdeas threads, and teambuildingworld. Reddit ranking on page 1 is a good sign that the query is winnable.
- Skipped queries where Google shows its own widget (coin flip, dice roller, timer, random number). Also skipped "new years eve raffle" (local lottery results), "thanksgiving trivia" (big sites plus a featured snippet) and Teams/Excel "how to randomly assign" (software intent).
- GSC (28 days to 2026-10-10): the site has about 0 clicks on all non-brand queries. Team/pair queries ("shuffle names into groups", "randomize pairs", "random pair generator classroom" at position 3) show demand for pairing content.

## Quick on-page wins (current pages)
Based on GSC (28 days) plus the live titles fetched 2026-10-13.

1. **/random-team-generator**: 35 impressions, 1 click, average position 35. Queries are "shuffle names into groups", "randomize pairs", "make random teams" and "divide names into groups". The current title leads with the head term only.
   - Title: `Random Team Generator – Split Names into Groups or Pairs (Free)`
   - Meta: `Paste names and split them into random teams, groups or pairs in one click. Reshuffle, print or share the result. Free, no account, and your list stays in your browser.`
   - Add an H2 "Randomly pair names" plus a short paragraph (to target pair queries), and a link to /guides/how-to-make-balanced-random-teams.
2. **/random-name-picker**: 62 impressions, 4 clicks, position 40. Long-tail queries ("giveaway name picker", "name picker randomizer").
   - Title: `Random Name Picker – Free Name Wheel, Remove Winner & Share`
   - Meta: `Paste up to 60 names and spin to pick one at random. Remove each winner, share the wheel with a link, and keep your list in your browser. Free, no account.`
   - Add a "Picking a giveaway winner" section linking to /raffle-generator and the giveaway guide.
3. **/prize-wheel**: 38 impressions, position 15, top query "prize wheel giveaway". Page 2 is within reach.
   - Title: `Prize Wheel Spinner for Giveaways – Free, No Signup`. Keep "giveaway" in the H1 and add a FAQ: "How do I use a prize wheel for a giveaway?"
4. **/yes-no-wheel**: position 13, query "yes or no spin wheel decide".
   - Title: `Yes or No Wheel – Spin to Decide (Free Yes/No Spinner)`
   - Meta: `Can't decide? Spin the yes or no wheel for a 50/50 answer, add Maybe for three options, and count results. Free, no account.`
5. **/secret-santa-generator**: position 1 already, but tiny volume. Before mid-November, add "draw names online / remotely / virtually" wording to the intro and a FAQ ("How do I draw names for Secret Santa remotely?"), to match the autocomplete demand above.
6. **Homepage and /spinner-wheel share an identical title and meta.** That's a duplicate and cannibalization risk. Make /spinner-wheel `Spinner Wheel – Make a Custom Wheel and Spin to Decide | ExesTools` (or canonical it to the homepage), and give the homepage a hub title such as `ExesTools – Free Spinner Wheel, Name Picker & Party Tools`.
7. **Cross-link the new guides from tool pages** ("Guides" box): raffle → holiday raffle guide; name picker and number wheel → White Elephant guide; Secret Santa → White Elephant (vs section). Add both to /guides as soon as they publish, and request indexing in GSC.
8. **Classroom spinner** (position 4, 30 impressions): the meta has "&amp;" and an em dash. Tighten it to `Free classroom spinner and random student picker. Paste your roster, spin fairly, and remove students once picked. Lists stay in your browser, no account.`

Claims used are limited to the approved list (free, no account, remove winner, share link, list stays in browser, presets, and features visible on the live pages: up to 60 names, multiple tickets, alternates, draw record, 1–1,000 range, without repeats, printable bingo cards and the Christmas list). No fullscreen, ad-free, weighted, CSV, OBS or certified claims.
