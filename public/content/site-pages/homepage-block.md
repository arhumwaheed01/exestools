## What ExesTools is

ExesTools is a small set of free tools for the moments when a group needs a decision nobody can argue with: who answers next, who wins the prize, which team you're on, who buys for whom. There are ten of them, from this spinner wheel to a Secret Santa generator, a raffle generator and a printable bingo card maker. Each one is set up for a specific job, with presets you can load and edit, so you're not forcing a general wheel to do something it wasn't built for.

## Who it's for

Teachers use the classroom spinner and team generator to call on students and form groups without favourites. Event hosts and streamers use the prize wheel and raffle generator for giveaways. Families and offices use the Secret Santa generator. Friends use the yes or no wheel to stop debating where to eat. If you need a fair pick in front of other people, it's for you.

## How a pick is made

Every result comes from your browser's secure random number generator, and the wheel only animates to the answer that was already chosen. Spinning longer or harder changes nothing. Switch on Remove winner if each name should come up only once. Read the full explanation in [How random is a spinner wheel?](/guides/how-random-is-a-spinner-wheel)

## Your list, your browser

There's no account to create. Whatever you type is kept in your own browser, and it only leaves your device if you send someone a share link, which carries the list inside it.

## How it stays free

ExesTools is built and maintained by one developer, Arhum Waheed, and paid for by ads from Google AdSense on some pages. That is what keeps the tools free to use. The names and options you type are never sent to advertisers.
